Practical Systems

TWO SYSTEMS THAT CHANGE HOW YOU HANDLE DIFFICULT DAYS.

SYSTEM 01

The Floor System

What happens when you don't have the time or energy to complete your usual routine?

Most people either do everything as planned or abandon the task completely.

The Floor System gives you another option.

Set your normal target, then establish a smaller minimum you can fall back on.

For example, work towards your one-hour study target, but use 15 minutes as your floor on a difficult day.

You still make progress without pretending every day will go perfectly.

SYSTEM 02

The Worst Day Protocol

Some days don't go according to plan.

You have unexpected responsibilities. Your schedule falls apart. Things become difficult.

Instead of abandoning everything, the Worst Day Protocol helps you:

  • Pause and assess the situation.
  • Identify what matters most.
  • Reduce your workload.
  • Maintain realistic minimum standards.
  • Recover and reset.

One difficult day doesn't have to become a week of abandoned commitments.
