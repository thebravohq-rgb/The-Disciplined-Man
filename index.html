WHAT NEEDS TO CHANGE

YOU CANNOT BUILD YOUR LIFE AROUND HOW YOU FEEL.

Some mornings you'll wake up ready to work. Other mornings you'll want to stay in bed. Some days you'll have plenty of energy. Other days you'll be tired, frustrated or distracted.

That's part of life. Your responsibilities don't disappear because you're having an uncomfortable day.

If you only follow through when you feel motivated, your progress will always depend on your mood.

You need habits you can maintain, standards you can follow and a way to handle the days when your plans fall apart.

That's what self-government is about: taking responsibility for your behaviour and learning to direct your own decisions.

PRACTICAL SYSTEMS INSIDE

WHAT DO YOU DO WHEN DISCIPLINE GETS DIFFICULT?

It's easy to make plans when everything is going well. The real test comes when you're tired, busy, distracted or dealing with an unexpected problem.

The book introduces practical systems to help you handle those moments instead of abandoning everything.

The Floor System

Set a normal target for the work you want to complete, then establish a smaller minimum you can maintain on difficult days.

For example, if your study target is one hour, your minimum might be 15 minutes when the day becomes difficult.

You still have work to do, but one difficult day doesn't automatically become a complete break from the habit.

The Worst Day Protocol

What happens when your schedule falls apart and you cannot complete everything you planned?

Instead of giving up on the entire day, you learn to pause, identify what matters most, reduce your workload and maintain realistic minimums.

The goal is to recover, reset and return to your responsibilities.
