Practical Tools

WHAT DO YOU DO WHEN THE DAY DOESN'T GO AS PLANNED?

This is where many routines fall apart. You miss your target, feel behind and decide there's little point continuing.

The book introduces two practical systems to help you handle those situations.

01 — THE FLOOR SYSTEM

Have a minimum you can fall back on.

Your normal target might be one hour of studying. But what happens when the day gets busy?

Instead of abandoning the task, set a smaller minimum. For example, 15 minutes.

You still work towards your goal, even when you cannot complete the full routine.

02 — THE WORST DAY PROTOCOL

Know what to do when things go wrong.

Some days will be difficult. Your schedule may fall apart and your original plans may no longer be realistic.

The protocol helps you pause, identify your priorities, reduce your workload, maintain realistic minimums and reset.

One difficult day doesn't have to become a week of abandoned commitments.
